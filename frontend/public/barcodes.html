Daumar Grocery Store | Barcode Test Sheet

High-resolution retail barcodes (EAN-13 / Code 128) for hardware guns & camera scanner

How to test scanning:
1. Open IsaacPOS Register at http://localhost:5173 in another window, or on your tablet / iPad / phone.
2. Point your USB/Bluetooth Barcode Gun or click "📷 Camera Scan" on the POS register and aim at any barcode below.
3. The register will play a realistic "BEEP!" and immediately add the item to your active order cart!
